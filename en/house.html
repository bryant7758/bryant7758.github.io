Mountain House

About the House

The house sits between Belleayre and Hunter, so either mountain is an easy drive. Three rooms, shared kitchen and living room — great for a family or a group of friends heading up to ski for two days.

  • Room A — Suite: Queen bed + bunk bed (Full bottom, Single top), private bathroom, sleeps up to 5.
  • Room B — Large Room: King bed + bunk bed (two Singles), sleeps up to 4.
  • Room C — Small Room: Full bed + Single bed, sleeps up to 3.
  • Large and Small Rooms share one large bathroom; the whole house sleeps up to 12.
