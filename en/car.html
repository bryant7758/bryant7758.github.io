Ski Carpool

About the Carpool

No driving, no early-morning bus meetup. We pick you up at your door and drop you at the mountain; after the lifts stop, we bring you back. Weekday day trips run daily; weekend trips are 2 days (Saturday up, Sunday back) at the same price as a day trip.

  • Belleayre: $90/person (round trip)
  • Hunter: $110/person (round trip)
  • Door-to-door pickup: Manhattan · Jersey City · Flushing · Long Island Exit 39
  • One van covers both mountains: Hunter first, then Belleayre; on the way back, Belleayre first, then Hunter.
  • Lift tickets not included, no meals. Belleayre uses NY State ORDA SKI3, Hunter takes Epic.
