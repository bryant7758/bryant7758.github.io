Private Ski Lessons

About the Coach

Every weekend from Thanksgiving through the end of the season, I teach ski and snowboard lessons at Belleayre Mountain. I ski here all the time and know the mountain and its trails well.

Lessons

  • Private lessons 1-on-1 / 1-on-2 / 1-on-3: come solo, with family, or with a few friends.
  • First-timers: never been on snow? We start from putting on the skis or board, standing steady, and stopping — step by step.
  • Basic turns: already sliding and ready to level up — turns, speed control, edge changes.
  • Ski and snowboard, both taught.

Message for prices — tell us how many people, your level, and ski or snowboard.

Lessons can be arranged together with the carpool and lodging: carpool up Saturday, ski home Sunday, stay at the mountain house and ski both days. Message us for details.
