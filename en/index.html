NYC Ski Carpool & Mountain House

2026-27 Season · Belleayre / Hunter

Ski weekends, made easy: door-to-door carpool up the mountain and a house between Belleayre and Hunter, so you can ski again the next day. Car and house are booked separately — no bundles.

Mountain House

Suite, Large Room, Small Room, or the whole house, from $100 on weekdays. Between the two mountains, shared kitchen and living room.

Ski Carpool

Belleayre $90/person, Hunter $110/person. Pickup in Manhattan, Jersey City, Flushing, and Long Island.

Private Ski Lessons

Private ski and snowboard lessons every day, at Belleayre or Hunter, on your schedule. 1-on-1 / 1-on-2 / 1-on-3, beginners and basic turns. Message for prices.

How the Season Works

  • Day trip: carpool up in the morning, ride back after the lifts stop — home the same day.
  • Weekend: Saturday up, Sunday back, stay at the mountain house and ski both days — same carpool price as a day trip.
  • Learn to ski: book a private lesson and our instructor will take you from zero at Belleayre or Hunter.

Lift tickets not included: Belleayre uses NY State ORDA SKI3, Hunter takes Epic. All times are approximate and adjust to traffic and pickups.
